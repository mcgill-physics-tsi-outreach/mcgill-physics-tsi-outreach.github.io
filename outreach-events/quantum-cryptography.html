Quantum Cryptography Workshop

October 14, 2025

On October 14, 2025, McGill Physics Outreach hosted a Quantum Cryptography Workshop for undergraduate students at McGill University. The event aimed to introduce students to the fascinating world of quantum mechanics and its applications in secure communication.

The workshop began with an interactive presentation on the principles of quantum mechanics, including superposition and entanglement. Students learned how these concepts form the basis of quantum cryptography, which promises unprecedented security in data transmission.

Following the presentation, participants engaged in hands-on activities that demonstrated quantum key distribution (QKD) protocols. Using simple optical setups, students simulated the BB84 protocol, allowing them to experience firsthand how quantum states can be used to generate secure encryption keys.

Throughout the day, students had the opportunity to ask questions and discuss the implications of quantum technologies with McGill researchers. The workshop also highlighted current advancements in quantum computing and its potential impact on various industries.

The event concluded with a group discussion on the ethical considerations and future challenges associated with quantum cryptography. Students expressed enthusiasm about the possibilities of quantum technologies and their role in shaping the future of secure communication.

Overall, the Quantum Cryptography Workshop provided students with a unique opportunity to explore cutting-edge science in an interactive and engaging environment. McGill Physics Outreach is committed to inspiring the next generation of scientists and fostering a deeper understanding of complex scientific concepts.
