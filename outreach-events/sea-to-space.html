Sea to Space (Science pour tous)

September 30th, 2025

On October 4th, TSI & McGill Physics Outreach teamed up with Science pour tous to host "Sea to Space," an engaging outreach event for over 200 local students in Montreal. The event aimed to inspire young minds by showcasing the wonders of physics and space science through interactive exhibits and hands-on activities. The hands-on activities featured demos about gravity, life on other planets, energy, planetary systems, and more!
