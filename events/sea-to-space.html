Dark Matter Day 2024

October 30, 2024 – Public Talks & Interactive Activities

On October 30, 2024, McGill University celebrated Dark Matter Day with an evening of science, discovery, and conversation. Professors Katelin Schutz and Yashar Hezaveh led captivating public talks on the nature of dark matter, cosmology, and the mysteries of the universe.

The event featured a Q&A session, interactive outreach demos, and hands-on exhibits prepared by our volunteers. It was part of our effort to make cutting-edge physics accessible and exciting for all audiences.
